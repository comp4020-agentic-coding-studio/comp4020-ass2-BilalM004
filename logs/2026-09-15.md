- Traced how `related:`/`links:` actually work (in `astro-course-university`,
  vendored under `node_modules`) while explaining it: `related:` is a
  frontmatter list of other content-node ids: declaring it on one side is
  enough, the graph build adds the reverse edge automatically, and a
  dangling target fails the build. `links:` is the separate field for
  external URLs (YouTube, papers) — rendered in the same block, never a
  graph edge, no reverse-edge or dangling-ref behaviour since it points
  outside the site.
