  tutors, each owning one fieldwork-only Crossing, plus a convenor who owns
  the two presentation Crossings). To keep all five voices genuinely
  distinct instead of just differing by job title, used JCU's Employability
  Edge "Personality and Personal Attributes" module
  (https://www.jcu.edu.au/employability-edge/you-and-your-career/personality-and-personal-attributes)
  as an authoring reference: gave each person a Big Five trait leaning and a
  Belbin team role, then translated that into how they actually sound on
  the page (e.g. Haskell Byte's Monitor Evaluator lean reads as systematic
  and helpful; Yu Tobe's Teamworker lean reads as someone who thinks in
  beats and hooks). The framework itself isn't printed anywhere on the
  site — it's design notes in `deliverables/people.md`, not site copy.
