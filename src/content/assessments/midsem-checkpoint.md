Crossing 3 is Set Up Shop: instead of one conversation or one trade, you
put something out in the hallway for anyone to stop at — and see who does.
It's the biggest undertaking of the first half, and the Hallway Showcase is
where that scale earns its own weight, separate from the ordinary weekly
grade every Crossing gets.

## The brief

> Set up something in the hallway bigger than anything you've tried so
> far, then present what actually happened at the Showcase.

Bigger means more exposure, not just more effort: something that runs
without you standing right next to it the whole time, that a stranger
could walk up to without you initiating. The Showcase presentation isn't a
recap of the week's live report — it's the fuller account, with room to
say what you'd built for and what actually showed up instead.

## What you present

A short presentation at the Hallway Showcase covering what you set up, why
you scaled it the way you did, and what came of it — good and bad. No
separate written submission; the Showcase presentation is the checkpoint.
Missing the slot itself is covered by [Policies](/policies/) — there's no
presenting it late.
