The `due` date above is when Crossings run until, not a single deadline —
each week's participation is due in that week, at the Crossing itself.

Every [Crossing](/sessions/) ends the same way: you tell the group what you
did that week, and what it got you. Ten weeks, ten chances to actually go
do something real — start a conversation, run a stall, ask a favour and
maybe not get it — and then stand up and say so, out loud, in front of
people who can ask you a hard question about it. Weeks 11 and 12 are
different: there's no hallway work left to report, so participation there
means showing up and presenting at the Legacy Crossing itself, and being
in the room for everyone else's.

The doing comes first. Reporting it well and reflecting on it honestly are
how the doing gets graded, but there's no report to give if nothing
happened that week.

## The brief

> Show up to every Crossing having done the thing, and having thought about
> what it cost you to do it.

Attendance alone isn't the bar, and neither is a polished story. "I meant to
talk to someone from the robotics club" doesn't clear it, because nothing
happened — and neither does a report that's clearly been rehearsed since the
thing actually happened. What does: naming who, where, what you asked or
offered, what happened, and what you make of it — while it's still close
enough to be honest.

## What you submit

Nothing written ahead of time. The report and reflection are spoken, live,
at your Crossing; the mark comes from being there and from what you say when
the room asks "what did that actually get you." Couldn't do the hallway
work itself that week? See [Policies](/policies/) — there's no reporting
after the fact, but there is a way to still bring something real to show
for it.
