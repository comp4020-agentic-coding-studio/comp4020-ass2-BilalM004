There's no hallway work in the last two weeks. Crossing 6 is Legacy — the
one built entirely out of the other five. What you leave behind isn't a
new conversation or a new stall, it's an account of the whole semester:
every bet you made, what came back, and the five clips that prove it
happened.

## The brief

> Walk the room through your semester, then play the video that backs it up.

Weeks 11 and 12 are the Hallway Finale: some of that time you're presenting,
the rest you're in the room watching everyone else's. The presentation
covers all five Crossings — Starting a Conversation through Building With
Others — not a highlight reel of the ones that went well. If something
fell flat, that's part of the account, not something to leave out.

## What you present

A short presentation, in person, covering the five Crossings and what each
one bought you, ending with your five-clip video played in full. No written
report is due separately — the presentation and the video are the
submission. Missing the Finale itself is covered by
[Policies](/policies/) — there's no presenting it late.
