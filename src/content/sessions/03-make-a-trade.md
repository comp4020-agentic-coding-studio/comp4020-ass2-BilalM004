Conversation was free; this Crossing has a price. Not a dollar figure —
money's banned on purpose — but a real one all the same: you have to put
something up first and see what the hallway thinks it's worth.

## The brief

> Trade something with a stranger — no cash — and see what comes back.

A busker between sets who could use a hand carrying an amp. Someone selling
badges at a stall who'd rather trade one for a favour than for coin. A
skill you actually have — proofreading, a language, fixing a bike chain —
offered to whoever looks like they need it that day. Object, favour, tip,
skill: pick the currency, name what you're offering before you know what
you'll get, and let the other person set the rest of the price.

## What you carry into next week

The trade itself, plus your honest guess at what it was worth going in —
[The Exchange Rate](/sessions/04-the-exchange-rate/) is where that guess
gets checked against what actually came back.
