No hallway work this fortnight — Legacy is built entirely out of the five
Crossings that already happened. Before that account goes in front of a
mark, it goes in front of the room one more time, while there's still
time to fix what isn't working.

## In the room

A full run of the presentation you're planning for the Finale — all five
Crossings, five-clip video included — in whatever state it's actually in,
not a version smoothed over for a dry run. The room's job is the same one
it's had all semester: ask the question that finds the weak spot before
the mark does.

## What you leave with

Not a mark — a note. Something specific enough to actually change before
[The Hallway Finale](/sessions/12-the-hallway-finale/): a Crossing that
needs a harder look, a clip that doesn't back up what you're claiming, an
account that's gentler on yourself than the room believed.
