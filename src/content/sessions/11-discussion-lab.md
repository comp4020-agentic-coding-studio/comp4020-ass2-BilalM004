No hallway work this fortnight — Legacy is built entirely out of the five
Crossings that already happened. Before that account goes in front of a
mark, it goes in front of your peers, while there's still time to hear
what doesn't land.

## In the room

No stage, no clock, no five-clip run-through — just small groups talking
through what each of you actually lived across the semester. Bring the
account you're planning for the Finale and let people ask into it: what
happened, what you'd do differently, which Crossing you're quietly less
sure about than the others.

## What you leave with

Not a mark — a peer's reaction. Something a stranger to your semester
noticed that you didn't say out loud until they asked: a Crossing that
needs a harder look, a claim your own account doesn't quite back up, an
account that's gentler on yourself than your peers believed once you
talked it through. Carry that into [The Hallway Finale](/sessions/12-the-hallway-finale/).
