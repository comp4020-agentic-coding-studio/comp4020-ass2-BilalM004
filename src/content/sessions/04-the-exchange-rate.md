Every trade has a rate, whether or not you did the arithmetic beforehand.
This week you do it out loud: what you put in, what you got out, and
whether you'd call that a fair exchange or a bet that didn't quite pay off.

## In the room

Report the trade the way you'd tell a friend who wasn't there — what you
offered, what they gave back, and the moment you realised whether it had
worked. "Fair" isn't the only acceptable answer; a trade that went badly is
just as reportable as one that didn't, as long as you can say why.

## What gets asked

Expect a follow-up aimed at the rate itself, not just the story: would you
make the same trade again, what would you ask for instead, did you get
the better end of it or the worse. See [Crossing Participation](/assessments/crit-participation/)
for how a live report and reflection turn into the week's mark.
