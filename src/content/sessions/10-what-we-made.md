Crossing 5 is the first one where the report isn't only yours — whatever
you made had two people's decisions in it, and the account should sound
like that, not like a solo project someone happened to be standing near.

## In the room

Show what got built, if it travels, or describe it if it doesn't, then
walk through how it came together: who suggested what, where you disagreed,
which choice ended up shaping the final thing. A report that only covers
your own contribution is missing half of what actually happened.

## What gets asked

Expect a question aimed straight at the "together" part — what your
partner would say if they were the one reporting, whether the finished
thing looks like either of your original ideas, what you'd have built
differently solo. See [Crossing Participation](/assessments/crit-participation/)
for how the mark gets made.
