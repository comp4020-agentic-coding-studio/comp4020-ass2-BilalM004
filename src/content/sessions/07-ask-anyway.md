Coming back from the break, the risk moves from what you're offering to
what you're asking for. Crossing 4 doesn't have a separate scale bar the
way Crossing 3 did — the risk is built straight into the ask, because a
real invitation can get a real no.

## The brief

> Ask a stranger to join you on a mini quest, decided on the spot — and
> risk hearing no.

Not a plan you made last night: something you decide in the moment, with
whoever's in front of you. Find the vending machine that's rumoured to be
broken and go check together. Ask someone to help you settle a genuinely
pointless argument. Invite them to the food truck you've never tried. The
quest matters less than the ask — small, real, made out loud, to someone
who has every right to say no and might.

## What you carry into next week

The ask itself, and the answer — yes or no, either one is reportable — plus
whatever you noticed in yourself in the second before you said it out
loud. [The Rejection Report](/sessions/08-the-rejection-report/) is where
that gets told. Get a clip — wherever the quest went, or just you
afterwards saying what the answer was. Fourth of your five for
[Legacy](/assessments/legacy/), and a no is as worth recording as a yes.
