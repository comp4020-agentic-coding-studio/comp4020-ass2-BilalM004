The last session of the semester. No fieldwork to report and no stall to
pack down — just twelve weeks of hallway bets, one presentation at a time,
in front of the people who watched you make them.

## In the room

Students present in turn, one slot each, every presentation ending with the
five-clip video played in full. Have the video ready to go: the slot is for
presenting, not for finding the file.

What goes in the presentation, and what it's weighed against, is on
[Legacy](/assessments/legacy/).

## The rest of the session

You're in the audience for everyone else's, and that isn't padding around
your own slot — it's the other half of what you're here for. The
[Discussion Lab](/sessions/11-discussion-lab/) the week before is where the
soft spots in your account were meant to surface; this is the version that
counts — and then you sit and watch everyone else's version of the same
twelve weeks.
