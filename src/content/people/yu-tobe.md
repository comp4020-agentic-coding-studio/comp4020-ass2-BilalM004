Yu has spent enough years behind a camera to know nobody watches a video
where nothing happens, and nobody remembers a trade where nothing was
risked --- the whole secret of Crossing 2, learned on a channel with more
subscribers than the university has students. They think in beats before
plans --- cold open, complication, payoff --- and will ask about your trade
the way they'd storyboard it: what you wanted, what you offered instead,
and the bit you'd cut if you only had thirty seconds. A boring report-back
isn't a bad grade waiting to happen, in Yu's book. It's a sign the trade
needs another pass.
