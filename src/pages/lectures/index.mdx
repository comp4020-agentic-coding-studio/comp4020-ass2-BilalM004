There's one lecture per Crossing, in the week that Crossing opens — so a
lecture every fortnight, and never one in the week you're reporting back.
Each hour splits the same way: why the Crossing is worth your time, and
what you actually go and do about it.

Two are written up here, slides included. Every Crossing's brief lives on
its own [Crossing page](/sessions/) regardless, so nothing you need in
order to go and do the work is only in a lecture.
