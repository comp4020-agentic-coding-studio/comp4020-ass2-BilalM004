There's one lecture per Crossing, in the week that Crossing opens — so a
lecture every fortnight, and never one in the week you're reporting back.

Every one of them is split half and half. The convenor takes the first
half: the idea, and why this Crossing is worth your time. That Crossing's
own tutor takes the second: how you actually go and do it, and what you owe
the room when you get back. For Crossings 3 and 6 those are the same
person, so the halves are marked out by what they cover rather than by who
is standing there.

All six are here, slides included. Every Crossing's brief lives on its own
[Crossing page](/sessions/) regardless, so nothing you need in order to go
and do the work is only in a lecture.
